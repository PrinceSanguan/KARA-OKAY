## Why Kara-okay

Many karaoke apps hand you a player and leave the rest to you: find song files, find a soundfont, make the right folders, copy everything over, and hope it works. Kara-okay does all of that for you.

- **Install, tap one button, sing.** Songs, instrument sounds and video backdrops download inside the app. No file manager, no folders, nothing to copy.
- **Sing before it finishes.** The first songs are ready long before the download ends. The rest keeps coming in the background.
- **Made for Filipino parties.** More than 47,000 songs, each one checked: OPM, International, Christmas, Worship, Bisaya at Ilocano, Medley, Reggae · Bossa · Bachata, Pambata at Tradisyonal, and more, all with lyrics, the latest hits included.
- **Works like the real thing.** Every song has a five-digit number, a printable songbook, a keypad and a reserve list. If you have used a videoke machine, you already know how to use it.
- **Your own files, if you like.** Add your own songs, karaoke videos and sounds, or plug in a USB drive. No limit on how many.

## Kara-okay vs SongHub

Kara-okay owes its start to **SongHub**, the father of karaoke apps on Android: without it, Kara-okay would not exist. Salamat, SongHub! 🙏

If you have used SongHub, you know it is a capable player, with extras like MP3 playback, Android TV and a phone remote. Its setup, though, is all on you. Kara-okay takes that setup away. It has Android TV, a phone remote and your own MIDI, KAR and MP3 songs too, and since 1.4.0 a Kara-okay folder you can fill with a file manager, the way SongHub users are used to.

| Getting to the first song | SongHub | Kara-okay |
|---|---|---|
| Song files | Find karaoke files yourself and copy them into a `SongHub/songs` folder | Downloaded inside the app with one tap |
| Instrument sound | Find an `.sf2` soundfont and copy it into `SongHub/sound` | Classic included, Major and Dreamsam one tap away |
| Folders to create | `SongHub/` with `songs/`, `sound/`, `bgv/`, `bgphoto/` and more | None (a Kara-okay folder only if you want one) |
| Backdrop videos | Copy your own into `bgv/` | Downloaded with the songs |
| When you can sing | After everything has been found and copied | Early in the first download, while the rest comes in |

*SongHub details are from its public project page. SongHub is a separate project with no connection to Kara-okay.*

## Install

### On a phone

1. **Open this page in Chrome** (not inside Facebook or Messenger). Open the [latest release](https://github.com/PrinceSanguan/KARA-OKAY/releases/latest) and tap **Kara-okay-1.8.0.apk** under **Assets**.
2. **Open the file** when it finishes downloading. If Android asks, allow Chrome to **install unknown apps**. Every app installed from outside the Play Store needs this once.
3. If **Google Play Protect** shows a warning, tap **More details → Install anyway**.
4. **Open Kara-okay** and, under **Ready to sing**, tap **Download songs**. Wi-Fi is best; on mobile data the app asks first. (Only want your own songs? Pick **My own songs**.)
5. When **Start singing** appears, you're ready. The rest keeps downloading in the background.

Already have an older version? Install the new one over it. **Your favourites, playlists and own songs stay.** Coming from 1.7 or older, the songs download once more for their new numbers.

### On a TV

For Android TV or Google TV:

1. Install the free **Downloader** app from the TV's Google Play.
2. Open it and type `github.com/PrinceSanguan/KARA-OKAY/releases/tag/v1.8.0`
3. Pick **Kara-okay-1.8.0.apk** and install it.
4. Open Kara-okay on the TV and, under **Ready to sing**, press **Download songs**.
5. To use your phone as the remote, scan the QR code on the TV's song list, or in its corner while a song plays.

### On a Windows laptop or PC

1. Open [Kara-okay PC 1.2.0](https://github.com/PrinceSanguan/KARA-OKAY/releases/tag/pc-v1.2.0) and click **Kara-okay-PC-Setup-1.2.0.exe** under **Assets** (124 MB).
2. Open the file. If Windows says **"Windows protected your PC"**, click **More info → Run anyway**. Kara-okay is new, so Windows doesn't know it yet. It is not a virus.
3. Pick **Install for all users**, and click **Yes** when Windows asks.
4. Click **Next** until **Install**, then **Finish**. Kara-okay opens and downloads the songs and sounds once (about 800 MB). After that, it works without internet.

**You need:**
- **Phone or TV:** Android 7.0 or newer, about 1.5 GB of free space (more with Major and Dreamsam).
- **PC:** Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), about 1.5 GB of free space.
- Internet for the first download only.

## FAQ

<details>
<summary><b>Is it free?</b></summary>
<br>
Yes, on phone, TV and PC. Download it, install it, and sing. There is nothing to buy. If you would like to give something back, tap <b>♥ Support Kara-okay</b> on the song list for the GCash code: any amount is appreciated, and nothing changes if you don't.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Is it safe to install?</b></summary>
<br>
Yes. Kara-okay needs no account and has no ads. On a phone, it asks for internet (to download songs and to talk to the TV on your Wi-Fi), notifications (to show download progress) and your OK to install its own updates. The microphone is asked for only if you pick the <b>Mic</b> score or record your singing; a recording is made only when you tap ● and stays on your phone. The camera is asked for only if you turn on the camera behind the lyrics: it is shown on the screen, never recorded or sent. Bluetooth ("Nearby devices") is asked for only if you use the Bluetooth remote. Access to your files is asked for only if you turn on the <b>Kara-okay folder</b>, and it never deletes your files or sends them anywhere. It never asks for your contacts or location.
<br><br>
On a PC, Windows asks once for your OK while installing. Kara-okay PC records when you press <b>Ctrl + R</b>, and for <b>Instant replay</b>: the last three songs sung with a mic on, so you can hear them again. Both stay on your PC and are never sent anywhere; turn Instant replay off in <b>★ Tonight</b>.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Windows says "Windows protected your PC". What do I do?</b></summary>
<br>
Click <b>More info</b>, then <b>Run anyway</b>. Windows shows this for new apps it doesn't know yet; it goes away as more people install Kara-okay. It is not a virus. If you want to check the file, its SHA-256 is on the <a href="https://github.com/PrinceSanguan/KARA-OKAY/releases/tag/pc-v1.2.0">release page</a>.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Does it need internet?</b></summary>
<br>
Only for the first download, about 750 MB on a phone or TV and about 800 MB on PC (Major and Dreamsam only if you ask for them), and to find an MP3's lyrics online when you ask. After that it works fully offline, on phone, TV and PC.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>My download stopped. Do I start over?</b></summary>
<br>
No. Tap <b>Resume</b> and it carries on from where it stopped. It also waits for the connection to come back on its own.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How do I update?</b></summary>
<br>
Kara-okay tells you when a new version is out and installs it for you, on the phone (from 1.1.0 on) and on the PC. On phone version 1.0.0, download the newer <code>.apk</code> from <a href="https://github.com/PrinceSanguan/KARA-OKAY/releases">Releases</a> and install it over the old one. Your downloaded songs stay.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How do I show the lyrics on a TV from my laptop?</b></summary>
<br>
Connect the TV to the laptop with an HDMI cable. Press <b>Windows key + P</b> and pick <b>Extend</b>. The lyrics go to the TV, and the buttons stay on the laptop. Want the sound from the TV's speakers? Click <b>Sound devices</b> at the top of Kara-okay and pick the TV.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can I add my own songs?</b></summary>
<br>
Yes. On a phone, open <b>Settings → Your songs and sounds → Add songs</b> and pick <b>MIDI, KAR or MP3</b> files, or karaoke videos (<b>MP4, MKV, WebM</b>). For an MP3, pick its <b>.lrc</b> lyrics file too, with the same name, and the words show while you sing. You can add a <b>.sf2</b> sound there too. Your songs are under <b>My songs</b>, numbered from 90001. On a TV, use <b>Send from your phone</b>. On a PC, just drag the files onto the Kara-okay window.
<br><br>
Have lots of songs? On a phone, turn on the <b>Kara-okay folder</b> in the same place. Kara-okay makes a <code>Kara-okay</code> folder with <code>Songs</code>, <code>Videos</code>, <code>Sounds</code> and <code>Backgrounds</code> in it: copy your files in with any file manager or a computer, and they play from where they are, with no limit on how many. A <code>Kara-okay</code> folder on a USB drive works too. SongHub's <code>.idx</code> song packs can't be read: point Kara-okay at the MIDI or KAR files instead.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can I turn my MP3 into karaoke?</b></summary>
<br>
Yes, since 1.5.0. Open <b>Settings → Your songs and sounds → Make an MP3 into karaoke</b> and pick an MP3 from your phone. Kara-okay takes the singer's voice out (<b>Voice: on / cut</b> in Controls while it plays) and finds the lyrics online, timed to the song; you pick the right one from the list. It works best on studio recordings; a live recording or a song with a lot of echo keeps some of the voice.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How do I put it on my TV?</b></summary>
<br>
On an Android TV or Google TV box, install the free <b>Downloader</b> app from the TV's Google Play. Open it, type <code>github.com/PrinceSanguan/KARA-OKAY/releases/tag/v1.8.0</code>, pick <code>Kara-okay-1.8.0.apk</code> and install it. Then open Kara-okay on the TV and download the songs once, the same as on a phone. Have a laptop instead? Use Kara-okay PC and an HDMI cable.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Does the singing score work on a TV?</b></summary>
<br>
Yes. Since 1.7.0 a TV gives a <b>For fun</b> score after every song with no mic at all. For a score from your real singing, pick <b>Mic</b> in <b>Controls → Score</b>. A TV has no microphone of its own, so plug a <b>USB microphone</b> into the TV box, or use your phone: open Kara-okay on the phone, connect it as the remote, and turn on <b>Score with this phone's mic</b>. Check it any time in <b>Settings → Singing → Test the microphone</b>.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How do I use my phone as the remote?</b></summary>
<br>
Put the phone and the TV (or PC) on the same Wi-Fi. The TV or PC shows a QR code: scan it with the phone's camera to reserve songs from the browser, with nothing to install. Or open Kara-okay on the phone, tap <b>Remote</b>, pick the TV or PC and type the 4-digit PIN shown on it. Some guest Wi-Fi networks keep devices apart; use the phone's hotspot then.
<br><br>
Guests can scan while someone sings: a small QR shows in the TV's corner during the intro and each interlude, and a big one any time from <b>Controls → Show the QR for phones</b>. Each guest's phone asks for a name once, shown on the TV with their song.
<br><br>
No Wi-Fi at all? Use Bluetooth: on the TV, <b>Settings → TV and remote → Bluetooth → Make visible</b>; on a PC, <b>Settings → TV and remote → No Wi-Fi? Phones can use Bluetooth</b> (pair a new phone with <b>Let a new phone find this PC</b>); on the phone, <b>Remote → Find by Bluetooth</b>. Up to 4 phones.
<br><br>
No TV? Since 1.6.0 a phone can be the stage too: on the phone that shows the lyrics, tap <b>Use this phone as the stage</b> on the song list, then connect the other phone the same way.
</details>

- [FluidSynth](https://github.com/FluidSynth/fluidsynth), the software synthesizer, under the GNU LGPL 2.1: version 2.6.1 on Kara-okay PC, linked dynamically (and on Android until 1.7, from the [fluidsynth-android](https://github.com/VolcanoMobile/fluidsynth-android) build).
- [GeneralUser GS](https://github.com/mrbumpy409/GeneralUser-GS) by S. Christian Collins, the basic sound bank under the others, under the GeneralUser GS License v2.0.
- The Major and Dreamsam sound banks are custom-made by JC of the Filipino karaoke community. The Classic sound bank is reSAM 16 by DoReMi Channel / Baklas Electronics.
- [Inter](https://github.com/rsms/inter), [Oswald](https://github.com/googlefonts/OswaldFont) and [Arimo](https://github.com/googlefonts/arimo) fonts, under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, and the lyric fonts Atkinson Hyperlegible, Poppins, Barlow Semi Condensed, Archivo Black, Sniglet, Lilita One, Arvo, Comic Neue and Pacifico (SIL Open Font License 1.1) and Permanent Marker (Apache License 2.0).
- [Jetpack Compose, AndroidX (CameraX included), Media3 and WorkManager](https://developer.android.com/jetpack), under the Apache License 2.0.
- On PC also: [FFmpeg](https://ffmpeg.org) (GNU LGPL 3, as separate DLLs), [miniaudio](https://miniaud.io), [Signalsmith Stretch](https://signalsmith-audio.co.uk), Freeverb, [Eclipse Temurin](https://adoptium.net) (OpenJDK, GPL 2 with the Classpath Exception), [Kotlin and Compose Multiplatform](https://www.jetbrains.com/compose-multiplatform/), [RtMidi](https://github.com/thestk/rtmidi), JmDNS, SQLite JDBC, Apache PDFBox, Apache Commons Compress and the QR Code generator library by Project Nayuki. The full list, with every licence, is in the `licenses` folder where Kara-okay PC is installed.

Since 1.8 the phone and TV app plays with [BASS, BASSMIDI and BASS_FX](https://www.un4seen.com) by un4seen developments (not open source), used unmodified.

Lyrics found online for your own MP3s come from [LRCLIB](https://lrclib.net), a free, open lyrics library, and only when you ask.

Song titles and artist names belong to their respective owners.
